## Purpose

This knowledge record preserves the pre-application development packet logic for Escambia County. It is intended for GrantBot planning, grant development, investor materials, partnership development, capital modeling and post-meeting updates.

## Project concept

- Nonprofit transitional/reentry housing village serving adults experiencing homelessness and adults returning from incarceration.
- Preferred physical model: code-compliant Pallet-style modular sleeping shelters, targeted at approximately 120 square feet each.
- Preferred operation: one adult resident per sleeping shelter.
- Communal private bathroom/shower facilities, laundry, case-management/program space and community room rather than a full bathroom in each sleeping shelter.
- Applicant is considering removal of the existing single-wide manufactured home if that increases lawful site capacity.
- Applicant wishes to retain one Silverback RV only if Escambia County confirms a lawful use. No long-term residential occupancy is assumed.
- Conceptual capacity discussion: 12 sleeping shelters plus two reserve locations labeled 13 and 14 solely to test the highest lawful site capacity. This is not an approved unit count.

## Official Escambia County pre-application requirements

The Escambia County Site Plan Pre-Application Conference checklist states that a package must contain at minimum:

1. Transmittal letter naming the project and submitted materials.
2. Project Information Form.
3. Narrative describing property size, number of units, building square footage, zoning, Future Land Use, water/sewer, wetlands, density transfers if any, access, stormwater and related development information.
4. Preliminary development plans drawn to scale, minimum 11 x 17. Engineer signature/seal is not required for the pre-application plan.
5. The posted checklist also calls for electronic copies on CD; applicant should confirm whether current County practice still uses CD or another electronic submission method.

The County checklist states that there are no fees associated with a pre-application conference.

## Local Pallet shelter precedent

The City of Pensacola Pallet Shelter Program and its published Pallet Shelter Development Cheat Sheet are useful local precedent, but do not control an unincorporated Escambia County parcel. City guidance states that multiple Pallet shelters may be treated as dwelling units within a mixed-use or multifamily residential development when zoning/density permits, and identifies building permits, licensed contractors, ADA, engineered anchoring, electrical/plumbing/mechanical permits, building separation, fire/life-safety, accessible routes, utilities and site engineering as relevant considerations.

The City guidance expressly tells applicants with property outside City limits to contact Escambia County Development Services for site-specific requirements.

## Communal hygiene concept

Pallet's current Two-Stall Hygiene product is approximately 100 square feet and provides two private lockable rooms; each contains a flush toilet, sink and shower. The manufacturer states the unit connects to electrical power, water and sewer. Accessible hygiene options are also available.

Broken Growth's preferred model is centralized communal private hygiene rather than a bathroom inside each sleeping shelter. The exact fixture count and facility configuration are County/Building/Health determinations.

## Rezoning decision rule

Do not file or pay for rezoning until County staff confirms rezoning is required or produces a material advantage for lawful project capacity.

The currently posted Escambia County Planning & Zoning Fee Schedule is dated October 2021 and lists reference fees including:

- Rezoning request south of Nine Mile Road: $1,530.
- Rezoning request north of Nine Mile Road: $1,305.
- Conditional Use: $1,530.
- Variance: $515.
- Site Plan - Major: $2,550.
- Site Plan - Minor: $1,025.
- Planned Unit Development: $3,860.

These are published reference amounts only and must be confirmed before payment.
