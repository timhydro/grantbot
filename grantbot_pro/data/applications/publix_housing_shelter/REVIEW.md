## Project Title
Broken Growth Transitional Housing and Reentry Stabilization Initiative

## Project Summary
Broken Growth Ministries is developing a transitional/supportive housing and reentry program for adults experiencing homelessness and people returning from incarceration in Pensacola, Florida. The project combines safe housing with stabilization, workforce development, job placement, mentorship, life-skills support, transportation assistance, and resource navigation so participants can progress toward income stability and independent housing.

The initial residential phase focuses on adult men age 18+ and prioritizes people experiencing homelessness and people returning from incarceration. Participants receive an initial stabilization period before employment or training expectations begin. The program is designed to reduce barriers that commonly cause people to cycle between homelessness, unemployment, and incarceration.
