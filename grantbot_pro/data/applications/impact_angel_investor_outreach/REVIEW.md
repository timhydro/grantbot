# Impact / Angel Investor Outreach — Review Packet

**Type:** Impact investment / angel investment / PRI / recoverable capital
**Status:** PROSPECTING + STRUCTURE REQUIRED

## Important Structure Rule
Traditional angel investors generally invest for equity or another financial return. A charitable nonprofit does not issue ownership equity like a normal startup. GrantBot must therefore route investor prospects into one of these structures before outreach:

1. Mission-aligned donation or grant to Broken Growth Ministries.
2. Program-related investment (PRI) or recoverable/low-interest loan to an eligible charitable project.
3. Debt financing to the nonprofit where legally and financially appropriate.
4. Equity/debt investment into a separately formed mission-driven for-profit social enterprise affiliated with the charitable mission, with legal/tax separation and conflict-of-interest review.

## Broken Growth Impact Thesis
Broken Growth Ministries is building a Pensacola-based housing and reentry platform designed to address homelessness, unemployment, and the difficult transition from incarceration back into the community. The model combines transitional/supportive housing, workforce development, job placement, mentorship, life-skills training, transportation support, and pathways to independent housing.

Potential earned-income activities could create employment and training opportunities in construction, landscaping, maintenance, food service, skilled trades, or other mission-aligned businesses. These activities may support a future social-enterprise investment structure if legally separated and operated with clear governance.

## Investor / PRI Pitch Draft
Broken Growth is seeking mission-aligned capital partners who want measurable community impact in housing stability, workforce participation, homelessness reduction, and successful reentry. Capital may be structured as a charitable grant, recoverable grant, low-interest program-related investment, community-development loan, or—where a properly structured affiliated social enterprise exists—an equity or debt investment into revenue-producing operations that support employment pathways.

## GrantBot Rule
Never offer equity in the nonprofit. Route ordinary angel investors to an eligible social-enterprise entity or convert the prospect to donation/PRI/debt outreach when appropriate. Flag securities, tax, governance, and conflict-of-interest issues for professional review before commitments.
