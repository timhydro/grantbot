## Project Summary
Broken Growth Ministries is developing a community-based reentry and homelessness-response program in Pensacola, Florida. The organization’s model combines transitional housing, stabilization, workforce development, job placement, life-skills support, mentorship, resource navigation, transportation assistance, and supportive community for adults experiencing homelessness and people returning from incarceration.

The planned residential model begins with adult men age 18+ and prioritizes applicants experiencing homelessness or returning from incarceration. Participants receive an initial stabilization period and then move toward employment, training, income stability, and independent housing. The long-term goal is to develop a replicable tiny-home/supportive-housing village model and expand services to women and families in later phases.
